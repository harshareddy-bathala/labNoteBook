## Abstract

A journaling app that maps thought connections as a living graph.

## Problem Statement

Linear journals lose context. You write something in March and forget
it connects to something you wrote in November. Search doesn't help —
you don't know what to search for.

## Hypothesis

A force-directed graph of journal entries, linked by semantic similarity,
surfaces non-obvious connections passively.

## Results

Archived after validating the concept with Haiku API. The graph
rendering worked. AI clustering was too expensive for offline use.
May revisit with a local Gemma model.
