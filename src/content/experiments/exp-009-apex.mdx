## Abstract

APEX was my attempt at a whole product in one go: a React 19 and TypeScript front
end, a FastAPI backend, MongoDB Atlas for storage and Gemini as the assistant. The
idea was to treat education as a social system, not a gradebook.

## What I took from it

About 127 commits in under three weeks, and the scope outran the polish: a lot of
surface area and not much depth in any one part. The repository is private and the
demo is still up.
