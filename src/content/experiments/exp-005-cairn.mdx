## Abstract

I built Cairn because my own placement-prep plan kept dying. It had no reminders, no
accountability, no curated resources, and it turned red the moment a day slipped.
Cairn keeps the roadmap and changes how time works.

## The idea

Progress is indexed by `day_index`, the days I actually closed a log, not by the wall
clock. A journey week is seven active days, so every quota corrects itself. Skipping
three days costs a streak and nothing else. A catch-up toggle lets one strong day
absorb two skipped ones, and a bad-day button shrinks the plan to a minimum chain so
the streak survives.

## What I built

- Next.js 16 on React 19 with Drizzle and Postgres, using Neon in production.
- Google sign-in through Auth.js, gated by an allowlist.
- Telegram reminders driven by a Cloudflare Worker cron trigger.
- The curriculum lives in typed TypeScript and is seeded into the database
  idempotently. **The database is a cache and git is the source of truth.** Seeding
  runs in one transaction and never deletes on its own.
