## Abstract

RootSense is hardware first. Node 1 sits in the field with a soil-moisture sensor and
a pump relay, and hosts the dashboard on its own access point. Node 2 sits in the
nursery with a temperature sensor, a light sensor and a shade motor, and broadcasts
its readings over ESP-NOW. Node 1 folds the nursery temperature into its urgency
score before it decides to water.

## What I learned the hard way

The repository opens with a wiring warning before anything else. ESP32 pins are
not 5 V tolerant, so the sensors belong on 3.3 V or the analogue readings clip and
the data line can be damaged. A flyback diode goes across the pump motor, and if the
board resets when the pump starts the motor needs its own supply with a shared
ground. All the calibration constants live in one config file per node.
