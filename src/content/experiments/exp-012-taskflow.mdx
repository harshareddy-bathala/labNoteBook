## Abstract

TaskFlow looks like an ordinary task manager. It has a passphrase gate, seeded demo
accounts, and a daily job that resets the database so a lab session can never leave it
broken. The flaws are deliberate and graded by difficulty, and the answer key is
kept out of the repository's public surface.

## Why it is private

A public repository would hand out the vulnerable source and the solutions together.
It stays private on purpose.
